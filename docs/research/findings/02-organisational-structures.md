## Topic 4 — organisational structures

**Confirmed**

1. (STR-01) Education Act s36(1): parents/guardians of children at a school constitute the **School Parents Assembly**; s36(2) the responsible authority must cause the assembly to establish a **School Development Committee**; composition/functions are in the assembly constitution. The statutory term is *School Parents Assembly*, not PTA.
2. (STR-03, STR-08) MoPSE SDC Handbook: government, non-government and satellite schools establish SDCs. At least nine members: five elected parents/guardians, School Head and Deputy *ex officio*, a senior teacher, and a Responsible Authority representative. Roles include infrastructure, resources, finance and conflict management.
3. (STR-02) Act definitions: "school" includes pre-school, primary and secondary school; "hostel" is boarding accommodation; "responsible authority" is the person/body responsible for establishment and management.
4. (STR-03) MoPSE School Leadership Handbook defines school leaders as **Heads, Deputy Heads, Heads of Department and Teachers-in-Charge**, and specifies duties of Senior Master/Lady, teachers and non-teaching staff. The Head has overall responsibility, chairs finance committee, is an account signatory, prepares budgets, keeps records, appraises teachers and implements the development plan.
5. (STR-03) Heads and deputies are Public Service Commission employees; MoPSE recruits school heads. Teacher appointment/dismissal is **not** the head's responsibility.
6. (STR-11, STR-12) In Association of Trust Schools/independent schools the **Board of Governors is the Responsible Authority**: it employs and appraises the Head, sets policy and delegates day-to-day running. The parent committee is a support group answerable to the Board; the SDC focuses on short-term improvement, the Board on long-term development.
7. (STR-13) One documented primary school lists School Head, Deputy Head, Teacher in Charge, HODs, Health HOD, ICT department, 44 teachers and 14 non-teaching staff including Bursar, Levy Administrator, Clerk, Librarian, Caretaker and Security; ECD added in 1995.
8. (STR-14) One documented secondary school lists Head, Deputy, Senior Master, Senior Lady, Bursar, Assistant Bursar, School Secretary, Examinations Officer, Estate Manager, Finance and Admin Manager, and HODs for Mathematics, Science, Humanities, Languages, Music, Practicals, Computers, Religious Studies and Careers.
9. (STR-16) A school policy defines School Head as "headmaster or headmistress who is the ultimate school authority"; its finance sub-committee includes Head, Deputy, SDC Chair, SDC Treasurer and Accountant.
10. (STR-09, STR-10) A satellite school is unregistered but authorised, attached to a registered "mother" school; the mother school's head oversees administration and a Teacher in Charge leads the satellite.

**Inferences (not confirmed)**

11. A likely hierarchy is Responsible Authority/Board → Head → Deputy/Senior Master/HODs/TICs → teachers, with the SDC as a governance/support body rather than a line manager. No national org chart was found; reporting lines must be configurable per school (**PROPOSED**).
12. "Campus" in the sources usually means a physical site; satellite and multi-site arrangements exist but no national campus data model was found.

## Topic 5 — ECD, primary, secondary and A-Level structures

**Confirmed**

13. (STR-04) UNESCO describes the structure as **2-7-4-2-3+**: two years ECD, seven primary, lower secondary grades 8–11, upper secondary grades 12–13; ZIMSEC administers ZELA at ECD, Grade 7, O-Level and A-Level.
14. (STR-05) MoPSE statistical nomenclature: **ECD A, ECD B, Grades 1–7, Forms 1–4, Lower 6, Upper 6**. Reported 2025 cohorts include Form 1 298,653; Form 4 229,966; Lower 6 40,500; Upper 6 37,413.
15. (STR-06) MoPSE curriculum modules: Infant School Module (ECD + infant grades), Junior School Module, through Upper Secondary (Advanced Level). Computer Science covers "the first four years of Secondary Education" (F1–4).
16. (STR-07) ZIMSEC exam-centre form lists **Grade Seven, ZJC, Ordinary Level, Advanced Level**; fee instalments begin at Grade 6 (Grade 7), Form 3 (O-Level), Lower 6 (A-Level).

## Topic 6 — classes, streams, departments and campuses

**Confirmed / documented instances**

17. (STR-05) MoPSE reports primary provision largely day schools; secondary includes boarding and day-and-boarding options.
18. (STR-15) A government high school offers Forms 1–6 and "Science, Commercial, and Arts streams" at A-Level.
19. (STR-17) A school document records a historical arrangement of "each stream (Form 1–4) having 12 classes to make it 48 classes in total".
20. (STR-13, STR-14) Department names and non-teaching posts vary by school; no standard national department list was found.

**Unresolved**

21. "Stream" is used both for A-Level subject pathways and for parallel classes. No authoritative national definition or count was retrieved. No evidence found for standard class sizes.
22. Grade ↔ Form crosswalk (Form 1 = Grade 8 etc.) is plausible from STR-04 and STR-05 but no explicit official crosswalk was retrieved.
23. SDC composition conflict: STR-03/STR-08 (≥9, five elected parents) vs STR-18 quoting earlier SI 87/1992 (five members). Current regulations must be checked before any rule is set.
24. ZJC remains on ZIMSEC forms but UNESCO describes a four-year lower secondary route to O-Level; its current curricular role is unresolved.

## Requirement candidates — PROPOSED only

- **PROPOSED PF-010:** Configurable school type including government/non-government, satellite and mother-school link (F3, F10).
- **PROPOSED PF-011:** Optional organisational units: site/campus → department → stage → grade/form → class → stream; only school mandatory (F14–F20).
- **PROPOSED PF-012:** Role catalogue with effective dates: Head, Deputy, Senior Master/Lady, HOD, Teacher-in-Charge, class teacher, bursar, secretary, exams officer (F4, F7, F8).
- **PROPOSED PF-013:** Governance bodies: School Parents Assembly, SDC with configurable membership, Board/Responsible Authority, parent committee (F1, F2, F6).
- **PROPOSED PF-014:** Configurable reporting lines and delegations, not a hard-coded hierarchy (F11, F6).
- **PROPOSED PF-015:** Stage model ECD A/B → Grades 1–7 → Forms 1–4 → Lower 6 → Upper 6 with grouping modules (F13–F15).
- **PROPOSED PF-016:** Qualification links Grade 7, ZJC (flagged legacy), O-Level, A-Level, ZELA (F13, F16).
- **PROPOSED PF-017:** Streams represented with an explicit, currently unresolved semantic (parallel class vs subject pathway) (F18, F19, F21).
- **PROPOSED PF-018:** Boarding/hostel and day/day-boarding attributes, without mandatory minimums (F3, F17).

None of these are approved requirements.
