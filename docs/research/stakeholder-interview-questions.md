# Stakeholder interview questions — PROPOSED guide

**Status:** PROPOSED. **No interviews were conducted in this milestone.** Any future interview must follow a consent process and its findings must be classified **INTERVIEW VALIDATED** (not OFFICIAL). Tracking issue: `P1-R19`.

## Interview protocol (proposed)

1. Explain purpose: research for an open-source system; no school data may be collected in the interview.
2. Obtain explicit consent; offer anonymity; record role and school type only at the level the participant agrees to.
3. Ask for the participant's own account, not "what schools usually do".
4. Ask for a document or public source where possible; record it separately as DOCUMENTED PRACTICE.
5. Ask permission to quote; do not publish names without written approval.
6. Capture disagreements rather than averaging them.

## A. School leadership (Head / Deputy / Teacher-in-Charge)

1. Which official document records your school's identity (name, EMIS code, registration number)? May we see a redacted copy?
2. How is your school classified in Ministry records — government, non-government, satellite, registered, provisional?
3. Which roles exist in your school today, and who reports to whom? Where is this written down?
4. How is authority delegated when the Head is absent? Is it time-bounded or revocable?
5. Which school-level settings actually change from year to year (terms, class naming, streams, departments)?
6. How are classes and streams formed, and who approves the structure?
7. What happens when a new school or satellite opens? Walk us through the steps and documents.
8. Where must records be kept, for how long, and who may inspect them?
9. What connectivity, device and power constraints affect record-keeping?
10. What would make a digital system unusable in your context?

## B. Teachers / Heads of Department / Examinations Officer

11. What academic stages and grade/form labels do you use, and where did they come from?
12. How do you prepare for Grade 7, ZJC, O-Level and A-Level examinations?
13. How are timetables, exam entries and learner lists produced today?
14. What information about a learner is sensitive, and who may see it?
15. Which tasks require a second person's approval?

## C. Bursar / finance committee / accounting assistant

16. Who initiates, certifies, approves and signs a payment in your school?
17. Are finance subcommittee decisions required to be unanimous? Where is that recorded?
18. Which records, cash books and asset registers must be retained, and for how long?
19. Who performs reconciliations and audits, and how often?
20. What segregation of duties exists between recording and authorising?

## D. SDC / School Parents Assembly / Board of Governors / Responsible Authority

21. What is the exact name of your governing body, and under which regulation or constitution does it operate?
22. How many members does it have, who is ex officio, and how are members elected or appointed?
23. How does it relate to the Head and to the Responsible Authority?
24. What decisions are reserved to the body and which are delegated?
25. How are minutes, finance reports and conflict-of-interest declarations handled?

## E. Parents / guardians / learners

26. What information about a learner do you currently provide to a school?
27. Who may view or correct that information, and how would you request a correction?
28. How do you receive important notices (paper, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice)?
29. What privacy expectations do you have for health, disability or family information?
30. If a child needs to exercise a data right, who acts for them in practice?

## F. MoPSE / district / provincial officials

31. Which identifiers must every school record, and which register is authoritative?
32. How are school status (registered/satellite/unregistered) and mother-school links recorded?
33. What is the official ED 46 / EMIS submission process today, including offline or Excel steps?
34. Which annual calendar is authoritative, and how are changes communicated?
35. What record-keeping, inspection and breach-reporting expectations apply to schools?

## G. Data protection / legal / POTRAZ-informed reviewers

36. Is the school a controller or processor for learner data, and is a licence required?
37. Which consent or lawful-basis evidence is expected for children's data?
38. What are the exact statutory deadlines and recipients for breach notification?
39. What retention periods are defensible for learner and financial records?
40. What cross-border hosting conditions apply?
